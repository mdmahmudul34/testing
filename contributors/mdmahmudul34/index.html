Adversarial perturbation, live

A small classifier looks at a synthetic image made of a few simple shapes and reports its confidence for each class. Slowly nudging pixels in the direction that most increases the "wrong" class score — an adversarial perturbation — can flip the prediction well before the image looks any different to a human eye. This is a simplified illustration of the fragility that motivates adversarial robustness research in ML security.
