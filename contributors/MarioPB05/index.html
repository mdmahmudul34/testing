Itacapp

Fundación SAFA · mar 2025 → today

School communication in one app: grades, attendance, schedules and a parent–tutor chat.

Ionic Angular Firebase ~1,400 daily users
+

A cross-platform app for the SAFA network that pulls school communication into one place: grades, attendance, academic follow-up, disciplinary records, weekly schedules, notification settings, and parents managing several kids from a single account. Ionic + Angular on the front, a custom PHP API built on FRAME (our department's internal framework) on the back, Firebase for auth, push and real-time data.

I joined during my internship and now I'm the one keeping it alive and growing it. The bits I'm proudest of: the full authentication flow bridging Firebase with our own API, a personalised push notification system, and a real-time chat between parents and tutors that people actually use. I also laid out the architecture so new modules can be bolted on, which is how the teacher module shipped recently with weekly timetables, assigned duties and follow-up tools.

02

Colette's Shop

School project · feb – mar 2025

A Brawl Stars-flavoured store where you buy random boxes and hope for a good brawler.

Symfony PHP MySQL
+

A gamified store inspired by Brawl Stars, built with Symfony. You buy random boxes hoping to unlock a brawler, earn gems towards future discounts, set an unlocked brawler as your avatar, keep an inventory of opened and unopened boxes, and rate the different box types. There's a proper admin dashboard behind it for boxes, orders and store stats, plus music and sound effects from the original game, because it felt wrong without them.

04

School Canteen Management

FUNDACIÓN SAFA · jan – jun 2024

Took the school canteen off paper: daily orders, allergies and cancellations.

PHP MySQL JavaScript
+

Admins configure service days, manage the daily orders, keep track of food allergies and handle cancellations through discount-code logic. It exports the key data to PDF, lets each school tune its own purchase and cancellation deadlines, and supports emergency menus. Not glamorous, but it removed a genuinely painful amount of manual work and made allergies much safer to handle.
